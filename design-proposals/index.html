Phase 1: Design Changes & Architectural Rationale

Detailed comparison of the existing website vs. the proposed Finalized Hybrid Design (Concept 1 + Concept 2):

Current Implementation Existing Baseline

1. Hero Visual Hierarchy:

Hero headline and call button sit alongside a ghost button ("Free troubleshooting guides"). The emergency urgency and historical authority are stated in plain paragraph text without dedicated visual anchors.

2. Three-Card Alignment:

Cards overlap the hero using negative top margins (-mt-12 sm:-mt-16). While functional, this causes varying heights and vertical crowding on medium screens (tablets and small laptops).

3. Emergency Action Visibility:

Emergency availability (7 days a week) is split between Card 3 and header. Homeowners in urgent crisis have to scan multiple text blocks to confirm immediate technician dispatch.

4. Company Identity & Authority:

The State of NH License #1 and 1936 founding are displayed in a modest left-bordered paragraph rather than celebrated as New Hampshire's foremost groundwater credentials.

Proposed Finalized Hybrid Concept 1 + Concept 2

1. Elevated Heritage & Trust Hierarchy:

Combines Fraunces editorial elegance with a 3-pillar verified credential bar (NH License #1, Founded 1936, 7-Day Service) directly below the primary CTA.

2. Equal-Height Staggered Triptych:

The 3 cards use natural flexbox stretch to guarantee identical card heights across all viewports. Individual staggered upward reveals (0ms / 120ms / 240ms) create a sophisticated entrance.

3. Clear & Tactful Emergency Access:

Live dispatch indicator ("Technicians On Duty Today") in the header bar and Card 3 gives anxious homeowners instant confirmation without turning the site into a garish siren.

4. Authentic Archival Framing:

Authentic rotary drilling rig photography is framed with subtle dashed inner borders, a floating "7 days a week" badge, and a photographic location caption plate.
